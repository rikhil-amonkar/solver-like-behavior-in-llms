## Key Observations

1. **Reasoning Token Usage**: DeepSeek uses significantly more reasoning tokens (10K-53K) compared to GPT-OSS (1K-3K) and Qwen (5K-6K)

2. **Caution A Effect**:
   - Generally increases reasoning tokens across models
   - Mixed impact on accuracy: helps DeepSeek (+2-6%), minimal effect on GPT-OSS and Qwen

3. **Task Performance**:
   - **Calendar**: High accuracy across all models (0.93-0.99)
   - **Meeting**: Strong performance for DeepSeek and GPT-OSS (0.90-0.93)
   - **BlocksWorld**: Lower accuracy for GPT-OSS (0.46-0.56), suggesting PDDL formalization is challenging

4. **Model Characteristics**:
   - **DeepSeek**: Extensive reasoning, benefits from caution prompts
   - **GPT-OSS**: Efficient reasoning, stable accuracy
   - **Qwen**: Moderate reasoning, consistent performance
